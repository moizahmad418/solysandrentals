1. Rental Terms

Rental Period: As specified in booking confirmation. Night Use: Allowed with headlights only. Age: Minimum 21; ages 21–24 may require additional deposit up to $250. Maximum Passengers: 4 persons per cart at all times, no exceptions.

Delivery: $50 fee for no-show or rescheduling with less than 24 hours notice. If Company delivery is delayed more than 2 hours, Renter receives pro-rated credit.

Extensions: Notify 4+ hours in advance. Unauthorized late returns: $100/day. Cancellation: 24+ hours before delivery: 50% refund or 75% credit (valid 6 months). Under 24 hours or no-show: non-refundable.

Steep Hills & Driveways: Some rental houses and condos are located on very steep hills and driveways. Please keep in mind that our carts are not 4-wheel drive or built to handle/climb some of these hills — this is especially true with a cart full of adults. We're here to help you plan routes that we know the carts can handle.

Only the Renter and drivers listed below may operate the vehicle. All must hold a valid license and be 21+. Renter is responsible for all authorized drivers' actions.

3. Vehicle Condition

Company will provide a vehicle in good working condition. Renter agrees to inspect the vehicle at delivery and note any pre-existing damage. Unreported damage may be charged to Renter.

4. Prohibited Uses

The vehicle shall NOT be used for: racing, stunts, or reckless driving; towing; exceeding passenger capacity; commercial purposes (ride-sharing, deliveries, paid tours); transporting hazardous or illegal materials; operation under the influence of alcohol or drugs; use by unlicensed or unauthorized persons; any illegal activity. The vehicle may NOT be subleased, loaned, or rented to any third party.

Violation voids all coverage and may result in immediate termination without refund.

5. Insurance & Liability

Included: Costa Rica's mandatory Marchamo insurance (third-party bodily injury and third-party property damage, on public roads only). Marchamo does not cover damage to the golf cart itself.

Optional Cart Damage Coverage:

Standard ($10/day): Limits Renter's maximum physical damage liability for the golf cart to a $1,000 deductible. Renter authorizes a maximum damage security charge of $500 on their credit card on file.

Declined Coverage: If no coverage is selected, Renter is fully responsible for the actual repair or replacement cost of the golf cart. Renter authorizes a security charge of up to $1,000 on their credit card on file to cover initial damage assessments, plus liability for any repair costs exceeding that amount.

Note: All coverage is strictly voided in cases of prohibited uses, reckless operation, underage driving, or intoxication.

Renter assumes responsibility for: vehicle damage beyond coverage, injury to Renter/passengers/third parties, and third-party property damage beyond Marchamo limits. Renter agrees to indemnify and hold harmless the Company from all claims, damages, and expenses arising from vehicle use. This indemnity survives termination of this Agreement.

Your deductible protection choice and credit card authorization are collected together below, in the Payment & Security Authorization section.

Charges to Card on File: Renter explicitly authorizes Sol Y Sand Rentals to process charges to the credit card on file for:

Repossession: Company may repossess the vehicle without notice if misused, abandoned, not returned on time, or involved in illegal activity. Renter pays all repossession costs including towing, storage, and legal fees.

GPS: Some vehicles have GPS tracking. Renter consents to tracking for safety and recovery. Data may be shared with law enforcement if required.

8. General Terms

Force Majeure: Neither party is liable for failures due to natural disasters, government orders, pandemics, or other events beyond reasonable control. Company will offer full credit (valid 12 months) or refund minus non-recoverable costs.

Governing Law: This Agreement is governed by Costa Rica law. Disputes shall be resolved in the courts of Santa Cruz, Guanacaste.

Entire Agreement: This Agreement and the booking confirmation constitute the entire agreement. Modifications must be in writing. If any provision is unenforceable, remaining provisions remain in effect.
