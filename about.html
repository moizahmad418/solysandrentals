At Sol Y Sand Rentals, we're more than just a golf cart rental company — we're your local partner for adventure in the beautiful Flamingo and Potrero areas of Costa Rica.

Our Story

Born in Flamingo, Built for Adventure

Sol Y Sand Rentals started with a simple goal: to help you experience the Gold Coast with absolute freedom and ease. As a partnership between a local Tico and an American expat, we bring together the best of both worlds. We've spent a combined 20+ years living, working, and raising our kids right here in the Flamingo, Potrero, and Brasilito community. Whether we're chatting in English or Spanish, our focus is delivering reliable, premium carts with the kind of hospitality only a true neighbor can provide.

We are deeply immersed in our local community, and we know these roads inside and out. Let us help you explore our backyard the way it was meant to be seen.
