Before You Book

Rental Requirements

A quick summary — full details are in the Rental Agreement you'll review when you book.

Valid Driver's License

Renter must hold a valid license and be at least 21 years old.

4-Person Maximum

Carts are limited to 4 passengers, no exceptions.

Authorized Areas Only

To keep you safe and our carts in peak condition, rentals are restricted to designated authorized areas — we'll confirm your destination works before booking.

Not Built for Steep Hills

Our carts aren't 4-wheel drive. Some hillside homes and driveways aren't accessible — let us know where you're staying and we'll confirm.

Payment

How You Can Pay

Credit Card

Select credit card on the Reservations form. No card details are collected on our website and we don't send payment links — your card is charged in person for the estimated total when your cart is delivered.

Cash

No payment needed now — simply have the estimated total in cash ready when your cart is delivered.

Bank Transfer

Choose bank transfer at checkout and we'll provide our account details, along with instructions to confirm your booking once payment is sent.

Cash Incentive Rate: To offset digital processing overhead, bookings paid in cash receive a streamlined, rounded rate. Please contact us directly if you would like to utilize this option.
Good To Know

How Pricing Works

Season Is Based on Delivery Date

Your rate is determined by the season your rental begins, applied across your full stay.

Longer Stays Cost Less Per Day

Rates automatically drop into a lower tier at 3 days and again at 7 days.

Need a Single Day in Peak Season?

Short 1–2 day walk-up rentals are available by phone or email, even when they're not bookable online.
